ABOUT

Born in New York City, raised in Croatia, now living in Denmark.

I moved to Denmark at 18, and my creative practice has been shaped by transition, belonging, and place.

I'm 24 and studying Film and TV Production at NEXT KBH in Copenhagen.

My work is rooted in atmosphere, memory, and visual storytelling — how light, silence, and place can carry emotion without explanation.
