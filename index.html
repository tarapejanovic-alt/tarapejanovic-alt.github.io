[ SLIDE 02 / ABOUT ]

ABOUT

Born in New York City, raised in Croatia, now living in Denmark.

I moved to Denmark at 18. My creative practice has been shaped by transition, belonging, and place.

I am 24 years old and currently studying Film and TV Production at NEXT KBH in Copenhagen.

My work is rooted in atmosphere, memory, and visual storytelling. I am interested in how light, silence, and place can carry emotion without explanation.

[ SLIDE 05 / OUTPUT FESTIVAL ]

OUTPUT FILM FESTIVAL

I organized two editions of Output Film Festival in Aarhus.

First year: 300 visitors, supported by Aarhus Soup. I coordinated volunteers, selected films, booked venues, and created promotional materials.

Second year: EU funding secured, 800+ submissions through FilmFreeway. Learned valuable lessons in delegation, problem-solving, and event management.
