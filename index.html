[ SLIDE 02 / ABOUT ]

ABOUT

Born in New York City, raised in Croatia, and now living in Denmark.

I moved to Denmark at 18 and my creative practice has been shaped by transition, belonging, and place.

I am 24 years old and currently studying Film and TV Production at NEXT KBH in Copenhagen.

My work is rooted in atmosphere, memory, and visual storytelling. I am interested in how light, silence, and place can carry emotion without explanation.

SELECTED WORK

[ SLIDE 05 / OUTPUT FESTIVAL ]

OUTPUT FILM FESTIVAL

I organized two editions of Output Film Festival in Aarhus.

In the first year, the festival welcomed around 300 visitors and received support from Aarhus Soup.

I coordinated volunteers, selected films, booked venues, maintained communication with artists, and handled promotional materials.

In the second year, we expanded the project and secured EU funding. We received over 800 entries through FilmFreeway.
