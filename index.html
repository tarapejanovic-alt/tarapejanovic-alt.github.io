[ 01 / ABOUT ]

ABOUT ME

Born in New York City, raised in Croatia, and now living in Denmark. I am 24 years old and currently studying Film and TV Production at NEXT KBH in Copenhagen.

I moved to Denmark from Croatia at age 18. Since then, my creative practice has been shaped by movement, transition, and belonging.

My work is rooted in atmosphere, memory, and visual storytelling. I am interested in how light, silence, and place can carry emotion without explanation.

[ 03 / FILM ]

BROKEN LIGHTER

In 2025, I worked as co-writer on the short film Broken Lighter, directed by Josip Gojevic at MetFilm School in Berlin.

I helped develop the script, shape characters, refine dialogue, and work on tone and comedic timing. This strengthened my understanding of character development, story structure, and collaborative writing.

[ 04 / OUTPUT FESTIVAL ]

OUTPUT FILM FESTIVAL

I organized two editions of Output Film Festival in Aarhus. In the first year, the festival welcomed around 300 visitors with support from Aarhus Soup.

I coordinated volunteers, communicated with artists, selected films, booked venues, and created promotional materials across social media and print.

In the second year, we expanded and secured EU funding. We received more than 800 submissions through FilmFreeway, teaching me valuable lessons in problem-solving, delegation, and event management.
