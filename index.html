01 / About

About me

Born in New York City, raised in Croatia, and now living in Denmark, I am a 24-year-old filmmaker and photographer currently studying Film and TV Production at NEXT KBH in Copenhagen.

I moved to Denmark from Croatia at the age of 18, and since then my creative practice has been shaped by movement, transition, and belonging. My work is rooted in atmosphere, memory, and visual storytelling.

My work spans film production, photography, and visual research. I am especially drawn to projects that combine personal observation with emotional detail and a tactile sense of place.

03 / Film

Broken Lighter

In 2025, I worked as co-writer on the short film Broken Lighter, directed by Josip Gojevic and produced as part of his final project at MetFilm School in Berlin.

I helped develop the script, shape the story, build the characters, refine the dialogue, and work closely on tone and comedic timing. The process strengthened my understanding of character development, story structure, and collaborative writing.

04 / Output Film Festival

Output Film Festival

I organized two editions of Output Film Festival in Aarhus. In the first year, the festival welcomed around 300 visitors and received support from Aarhus Soup.

I was responsible for coordinating volunteers, communicating with artists, selecting films, booking venues, and creating promotional materials across social media and print.

In the second year, we expanded the project and secured EU funding through our network at Ungdoms Kulturhuset. We launched a wide open call for submissions on FilmFreeway, receiving more than 800 entries.
