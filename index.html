About

I'm Tara Pejanovic, a 24-year-old filmmaker and photographer based in Copenhagen. I was born in New York, grew up in Croatia, and moved to Denmark when I was 18. Since then I've worked many random jobs, learned Danish at VIA University, and worked on two film festivals along the way.

Right now I'm studying film and TV production at NEXT København, getting hands-on with camera, sound, editing, and production. I love writing, reality television and learning new things.

Film

Broken Lighter: Co-writer

The video here is a clip from the film, not the full short.

In 2025, I co-wrote the short film Broken Lighter, directed by Josip Gojevic as his final project at MetFilm School in Berlin. Together we developed the script, shaped the story, built the turning points and characters, and polished the dialogue with a focus on comedy and tone.

The film was shot and edited in Berlin. The process showed me how collaborative screenwriting can be, and it deepened my understanding of character development, story structure, and comic writing. I was also able to draw on my experience writing stand-up comedy.

Output Film Festival

Role: Organizer. Years: 2023 and 2024.

I organized two film festivals in Aarhus under the name Output Film Festival. In the first year, we received support from Aarhus Soup and welcomed 300 visitors. We handled volunteer coordination, communication with artists, film selection, venue booking, and marketing, and created promotional material for Facebook and Instagram, plus posters and flyers.

In the second year, we grew our budget through our personal network at Ungdoms Kulturhuset, where we secured EU funding and ran a large open call on FilmFreeway with over 800 submissions. The experience taught me a lot about problem-solving, delegation, and organizing large events.
