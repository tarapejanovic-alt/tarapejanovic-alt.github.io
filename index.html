About

I move between film, photography, and memory.

I moved to Denmark from Croatia at the age of 18, and since then my creative practice has been shaped by transition, belonging, and the feeling of moving between places. I am interested in how atmosphere can carry a story without needing to explain itself.

My work spans film production, photography, and visual research. I am especially drawn to projects that combine personal observation with emotional detail — whether that means a short film, a still image, or an analogue photobook shaped by memory and place.

Photography

Aarhus, 2023.

During my time at the photography program at Byenshøjskole in Aarhus, I developed a strong interest in atmosphere, motion, and the way an image can hold emotion through imperfection. These photographs reflect a period of experimentation — a time spent learning how light, blur, and composition shape feeling.

Festival 2023–2024

Output Film Festival

I organized two editions of Output Film Festival in Aarhus. In the first year, the festival welcomed around 300 visitors and received support from Aarhus Soup. I was responsible for coordinating volunteers, communicating with artists, selecting films, booking venues, and creating promotional materials across social media and print.

In the second year, we expanded the project and secured EU funding through our network at Ungdoms Kulturhuset. We launched a wide open call for submissions on FilmFreeway, receiving more than 800 entries. The experience taught me a great deal about problem-solving, delegation, and managing larger creative projects.

Short film 2025

Broken Lighter

In 2025, I worked as co-writer on the short film Broken Lighter, directed by Josip Gojevic and produced as part of his final project at MetFilm School in Berlin. I helped develop the script, shape the story, build the characters, refine the dialogue, and work closely on tone and comedic timing.

The process strengthened my understanding of character development, story structure, and collaborative writing. It also allowed me to draw on my experience in stand-up comedy and bring that rhythm and timing into screenwriting.
