About

I’m a 24-year-old filmmaker and photographer based in Copenhagen. I was born in New York, grew up in Croatia, and moved to Denmark when I was 18. Since then I’ve worked many random jobs, learned Danish at VIA University, and worked on two film festivals along the way.

Right now I’m studying film and TV production at NEXT København, getting hands-on with camera, sound, editing, and production. I love writing, reality television and learning new things.
